Baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.

A good spot makes tummy time safer and easier for both of you. The floor is often the best place.

Never leave your baby alone on their tummy, not even for a moment. If you need to step away, take your baby with you.

Talk to your baby's care team if you are not sure where to do tummy time at home.
