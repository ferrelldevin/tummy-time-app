Baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.

Babies born early often reach skills later than babies born on time. That is expected.

Corrected age helps you compare your baby to what is usual for their due date, not their birthday.

When this app shows your baby's corrected age, it is a rough range from your answers. It is not a medical measure.

Talk to your baby's care team about what to expect for your baby.
