Baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.

Many babies fuss during tummy time, especially at first. Tummy time is hard work for a small baby.

It is also hard for you. If you feel upset, it is OK to stop, take a breath, and try again later.

Use the "Baby crying?" button any time for quick ideas.

Talk to your baby's care team if your baby cries a lot during tummy time.
