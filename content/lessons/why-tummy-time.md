Baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.

"Back to sleep, tummy to play." Your baby sleeps on their back. When your baby is awake and you are with them, some time on the tummy helps them grow.

Babies born early may need more time to get used to it. That is normal. Every minute counts.

Talk to your baby's care team about when your baby can start.
