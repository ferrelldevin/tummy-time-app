Baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.

The first tries may be very short. That is OK. Your baby is learning something new.

A good time is after a diaper change or after a nap. Ask the care team how soon after a feeding.

Log each try in the app. You will see the minutes add up.

Talk to your baby's care team about how much tummy time is right for your baby.
