# Tummy Time: content style guide
Updated: Nov 9, 2026. For everything a caregiver reads or hears in the app. Team proposal until the client approves it.

## Reading level
- 6th grade (AMA) to 8th grade (NIH) on every lesson, button and message.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Everyday words.
- Video first, with a picture for each step.

## Tone
- Calm, warm, practical. Many NICU parents are stressed or low; never add worry.
- Talk to the caregiver as "you" and to the baby as "your baby".
- Praise effort: "Every minute counts." Never blame: no "you should have".
- Expect fussing and say so: "Fussing is normal at first."

## Inclusive
- Don't assume who the caregiver is. Say "caregiver" or "you", not "Mom".
- Show different families, skin tones and home setups in images.
- Captions and a transcript on every video.
- Write text as separate strings so it can be translated later.
- No idioms or slang that won't translate.

## Safety wording
- Lead every tummy time lesson with the basics: baby awake, always watched, on a firm, flat surface.
- Use corrected age for milestones, and say what that means the first time.
- Warning signs and "when to call" wording come from the client's clinicians. Anything the team drafts is a placeholder until signed off.
- Emergencies get one plain line: "Trouble breathing or turning blue: call 911."
- Every lesson ends with where to get help: "Talk to your baby's care team."

## Not medical advice
- The app teaches; it never diagnoses or treats.
- Say "may" and "talk to your care team", not "your baby has".
- Show the note "This app does not replace advice from your baby's care team." on sign-up and the "when to call" card.

## Words
| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| born early | premature (fine in titles only) |
| your baby's care team | your provider, your physician |
| corrected age | adjusted gestational age |
| flat spot on the head | plagiocephaly, brachycephaly |
| head always turns one way | torticollis (unless explained) |
| quick check | quiz, test, score |

## Lesson template (from the build plan)
- Title, goal, 2 to 4 min video, text at 6th to 8th grade, 1 or 2 check questions, which pathway, clinician who approved it.

## Check before publishing
- Reading level score.
- AHRQ PEMAT (understandable and actionable) and the CDC Clear Communication Index.
- Clinician sign-off recorded.
