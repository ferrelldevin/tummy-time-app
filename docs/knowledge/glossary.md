# Tummy Time: glossary
Updated: Nov 9, 2026.

## Baby care
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Preemie | A baby born early, before 37 weeks |
| Tummy time | Awake, watched time on the baby's tummy; builds neck, shoulder and motor strength |
| Corrected age | Age since birth minus weeks born early. Used for a preemie's milestones until age 2 |
| NICU | Neonatal intensive care unit |
| Flat head | Flat spot from lying the same way (plagiocephaly, brachycephaly) |
| Torticollis | Tight neck muscle; the head keeps turning one way |
| Container baby | Too much time in car seats, swings and bouncers |
| Reflux | Spit-up after feeds; tummy time works better in more upright positions |

## Product
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Caregiver | Parents and family at home first; professionals second |
| Intake | Short sign-up questions that pick a caregiver's lessons |
| Pathway / branch | Lessons added to the shared core based on intake answers |
| Knowledge check | 1 or 2 friendly questions after a lesson; no pass or fail |
| "When to call" card | Safety screen, one tap from anywhere, clinician-approved |
| Drop | A small working release shown to the client |
| PWA | Progressive web app: a website that installs from a link and works offline |
| CMAI Tracker | The lab's existing app; the model for this one |

## Research and rules
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| IRB | Institutional review board; approves research on people before data is collected |
| Exempt | An IRB category for low-risk research. The IRB decides, not the team |
| PII | Personally identifiable information |
| PROMIS Anxiety | Short, free anxiety questionnaire; wording can't be changed |
| PSS | Parental Stress Scale; 18 items; free with developer permission |
| HIPAA | US health privacy law for providers, plans and their partners |
| FERPA | US student records law; likely not relevant |
| REDCap | ODU's research survey platform |
| MIDAS | ODU's account sign-in; caregivers won't have one |
| WCAG 2.1 AA | Web accessibility standard ODU must meet by Apr 26, 2027 |
